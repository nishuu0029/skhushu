Dear Khushuu,

Pata nahi tumhe ye website dekh kar kitni smile aayegi, lekin isse banate waqt mere face par definitely ek smile thi.

Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon ki life me kuch log bas aate hain aur chale jaate hain, aur kuch log aise hote hain jo life ka ek beautiful part ban jaate hain. Tum mere liye wahi ho.

Tumhari ek smile mera mood change kar sakti hai, tumhari ek baat mere poore din ko better bana sakti hai, aur tumhara hona hi mere liye ek beautiful feeling hai.

Mujhe nahi pata future me humari story ke kitne chapters honge, lekin ek cheez zaroor pata hai — main har beautiful chapter me tumhe apne saath dekhna chahta hoon.

Thank you meri life me hone ke liye. Thank you meri smile ka reason banne ke liye. Aur thank you bas tum hone ke liye.

I really love you so much, baccha. Shayad words kabhi properly explain hi nahi kar paayenge ki tum mere liye kitni special ho.

Always yours
Ksagar ❤️
